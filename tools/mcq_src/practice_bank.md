## Cell Injury and Necrosis

*15 questions*

### SBA

**1. The earliest manifestation of almost all forms of cell injury is** `Relevant`

- a) Nuclear pyknosis
- b) Cellular swelling
- c) Plasma membrane rupture
- d) Karyolysis
- e) Dystrophic calcification

**Ans: b) Cellular swelling**

**2. The hallmark of irreversible cell injury is** `Relevant`

- a) Cellular swelling
- b) Loss of microvilli
- c) Severe plasma membrane and mitochondrial damage
- d) Fatty change
- e) Detachment of ribosomes

**Ans: c) Severe plasma membrane and mitochondrial damage**

**3. Amorphous densities in mitochondria on electron microscopy indicate** `Relevant`

- a) Reversible injury
- b) Irreversible injury
- c) Apoptosis
- d) Adaptation
- e) Autophagy

**4. The most common cause of cell injury is** `Relevant`

**6. Respiratory acidosis occurs in** `Relevant`
